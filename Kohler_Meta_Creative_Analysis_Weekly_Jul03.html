What the Data Is Saying

What's Working This Week

  • Kitchen Sinks dominate — StriveDoubleBowl Video (2.66×) and StriveSingleBowl Static (10.65×) are the clearest scaling signal in the account. Both products convert from both formats.
  • DPA outperforms prospecting statics. The Lookalike DPA at ₹1,273 CPP is 36% cheaper than the best static CPP. Proven audience + dynamic product = lower CPP.
  • CategoryPLP Video (₹7,240 spend, 5.46× ROAS) is an underrated hero. High CTR (5%), reasonable CPP ₹2,413 — this format is working for mid-funnel Kitchen intent. Deserves 3× the budget.
  • High CTR doesn't require high completion. StriveSingleBowl Video has 5.82% CTR with only 0.60% P100 — users click from the thumbnail. Don't optimise for completion on DR campaigns.

What Needs Fixing

  • Sale creatives in BAU are bleeding spend. BoldDays Malleco (₹25k, 0.56× blended ROAS) was built for promotional pricing. Running it at full price just trains Meta to show Kohler ads to non-buyers.
  • Showering has no working creative right now. ToobiShowerFaucet Static burned ₹42k at 0.77×. The category needs a fresh concept — either a short-form video hook (15s, product in use) or a testimonial static.
  • Remarketing DPA is at 20× frequency. Once frequency passes 25×, CPMs inflate sharply. Expand the audience window from 60 to 90 days this week — don't wait for next Monday.
  • Top40 has ₹9.2k wasted on ads with 0 purchases and no ROAS signal (RainduetMFFilteration ₹3,978, AtomPullDown ₹1,720, EmilePullDown ₹1,158). Pause all at 0 purchases past ₹1,500 spend.
