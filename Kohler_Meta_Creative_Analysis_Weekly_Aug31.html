Creative Patterns

What's Working

  1. BoldDays sale-tagged creatives post the strongest ROAS band in the account (3.2x–5.1x) — urgency and discount framing is converting.
  2. Video beats Static and DPA on ROAS (2.47x vs 2.08x vs 1.49x), even though it isn't the cheapest CPM.
  3. Toilets is the most efficient category — 2.72x ROAS and the account's best ATC→Purchase rate at 21.7%.
  4. The 60-day remarketing pool remains the single highest-value audience at 3.0x ROAS on ₹46,702 spend, even as frequency nears saturation.

What Needs Fixing

  1. Kitchen Sinks & Faucets is 56% of total spend at a middling 1.94x ROAS — the account's largest efficiency drag in absolute rupees.
  2. Top 40 Products is the weakest category in the account at 0.42x ROAS — most of this spend is not returning.
  3. Video completion is poor outside the Toilet health-faucet creative — four of six tracked video ads show under 1% full-view rate, meaning purchases are coming from the hook, not the story.
  4. HighAOV DPA (₹29,978 spend) sits at 0.93x ROAS — the catalogue isn't finding a high-value audience yet.
