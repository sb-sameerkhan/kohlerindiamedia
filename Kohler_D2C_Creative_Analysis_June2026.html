🎨 Creative Signals — What's Working

1
Toilet Bundle creative has the highest CTR in the account at 6.91%. This tells you the bundle angle (product + accessories + price point together) is cutting through far better than single-product creatives. Apply bundle creative logic to Kitchen and Showering.
2
Lookalike AllProducts: CTR 4.77% with ROAS 2.53×. This creative set is doing both — high click-through and actual purchase conversion. The all-product range angle appears to be driving genuine intent. Creative is healthy; scale the audience.
3
CategoryPLP KitchenSink&Faucet: CTR 5.34%. Strong creative engagement even with interests targeting. The creative is compelling but the audience is wrong (interests users don't convert). Move this creative to Advantage+ targeting.
4
Google HealthFaucet PMax: CTR 7.72% — highest click-through on Google. Small budget but excellent early signal. The category is purchase-ready (CPA ₹2,980). Increase budget 3-4× to find the optimal spend level.

⚠️ Creative Signals — What's Failing

1
Remarketing DPA frequency at 73×. The same user is seeing the DPA ad 73 times on average. ROAS is still 4.49× but this will collapse. The remarketing audience pool is near-exhausted. Either widen the window, reduce daily frequency cap, or rotate creative immediately.
2
Basins creative: 39L impressions, 4 purchases, ROAS 0.46×. Reach is enormous but nothing is converting. The creative may be too generic or not showcasing the product's premium positioning. This is not a targeting problem — it's a creative relevance problem.
3
BoldDays KitchenFaucet PMax on Google: CPA ₹5,135 with only 23 conversions on ₹1.16L spend. The sale-specific creative assets uploaded to PMax are not generating purchase signals. Either the creatives aren't triggering purchase intent or the landing page isn't converting sale traffic.
4
Kitchen_Sink&Faucet Prospecting: 0.57% CTR with 12M impressions. The creative is broadly distributed but failing to generate thumb-stop attention. With ₹2L spend and 2.39× ROAS the funnel is working, but creative refresh needed to maintain efficiency as frequency climbs.
