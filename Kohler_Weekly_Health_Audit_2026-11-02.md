2. **Google Shopping conversion rate and AOV broke together.** 24 conversions at AOV ₹4.4k versus 89 at ₹9.8k. Check Google Merchant Center diagnostics for disapprovals and stock status on the top 20 products this week.
3. **Meta D2C click-to-page-view leak.** KitchenSinks 13% and Showering 7% of link clicks load a landing page. Toilets runs at 54%, Faucets Low AOV at 49%. A 6–7x gap across campaigns on the same site points to placement and creative (accidental clicks from Reels/Audience Network, cheap CPM video) rather than a site fault. ## 6. Bidding & Optimisation Strategy

- **Google Shopping on tROAS** is the likely driver of the throttle. After a weak stretch the algorithm cuts reach to protect the target. Check the target value against the 28-day ROAS actual. If the target is above the achieved ROAS, drop it by 15–20% for 7 days, or switch to Maximize Conversion Value with no target while the feed and tracking are verified.
- **Demand Gen on tROAS with inflated conversions** is learning the wrong thing. Pause the six Demand Gen campaigns' bid target (move to Maximize Clicks with a CPC cap) or pause spend until the conversion action is corrected. Setting a ₹-value tROAS against engagement events will keep rewarding junk traffic.
- **PMax Kitchen Faucet, Bidet, Focus Products, Health Faucet** run Maximize Conversion Value with ROAS of 0–0.22. Set a tROAS floor equal to the account's break-even ROAS or consolidate the four into one asset-group-segmented campaign so conversion data pools.
- **Brand Search** at Maximize Conversion Value with 96% IS is at ceiling. Spend of ₹1.18L a week to protect brand terms is acceptable but "kohler" and "kohler india" take 87% of that. Add a Target Impression Share cap or test a 15% budget cut and watch conversions.
- **Meta Lead Gen**: all campaigns appear to optimise for leads (Instant Form). Test a "Conversion Leads" (quality-optimised) campaign on PanIndia Toilet, which has enough volume (400 leads a week), to bias delivery to higher-QL profiles.
- **Meta D2C**: 16 ad sets were flagged as Learning Limited in the Oct 19 audit. Consolidate low-volume ad sets so each gets about 50 purchases a week; Faucets Low AOV (12 purchases) and KitchenSinks (9) are well short.
