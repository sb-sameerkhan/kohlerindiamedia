What's working

  1. Remarketing is the efficiency engine. The 60-day ATC DPA took 12% of spend and 20% of purchases (49) at 4.18× and ₹1,090 CPP, but runs at 12.7× frequency.
  2. Toilets lead prospecting. Ove One Piece Video returned 4.69× on ₹45,285 and 16 purchases; the Toilets campaign blends 3.34× on ₹80,396.
  3. Low-spend winners exist. Cuff Health Faucet Static (10.24×, CPP ₹237) and Malleco Pull-Down Video (3.96×, CPP ₹288) are efficient on under ₹3,000 each. Samples are 12 and 10 purchases, so scale in steps.
  4. Same product, different creative, different result. Beam Kitchen Faucet Static_1 runs 3.37× while Static_2 runs 0.31×. Emile GIF runs 1.32× against 0.38× for Emile Video.

What needs fixing

  1. Kitchen Faucets High AOV is 34% of budget at 1.02×. ₹1,50,368 spent for 94 purchases. Emile GIF alone is ₹95,112; Emile Video adds ₹37,211 at 0.38×.

  3. Category PLP Video reaches but does not click. 1.52M impressions, 1,201 link clicks (0.08%), 0.42× on ₹44,753.
  4. Checkout tracking looks incomplete. 133 initiate-checkout events against 248 purchases, and only 4 ATC logged for 10 Malleco Video purchases. Verify pixel events before trusting ATC-based ratios.
