What Needs Fixing

  1. Emile Pull-Down GIF. The biggest spender at ₹61,120 (17% of account), 9 purchases, 0.77× ROAS. Cutting it alone recovers the most wasted money.
  2. Beam Kitchen Faucet statics (Static_1 and Static_2). ₹46,363 for 6 purchases at 0.25× and 0.06×. Same product, same creative family, both failing.
